The Cultural and Beautification Association of Tarampados, Tinos, announces a competitive tender for the lease of a building and its plot within the village of Tarampados, Tinos.

The property includes a **40.75 sq. m. ground-floor building**, a courtyard, the basement toilets, and one of the plot's two outdoor areas.
Permitted uses are a food service establishment, a grocery store, or a similar business.

### Key tender details

- **Deadline to express interest:** November 6, 2026
- **Tender date and time:** November 8, 2026, at 15:00
- **How to apply:** email [tarampados@gmail.com](mailto:tarampados@gmail.com)
- **Venue:** online via Microsoft Teams
- **Minimum monthly rent:** €350
- **Bid increment:** €25 per offer

A **€700 participation deposit** is required to finalize participation, in accordance with the tender terms.

The lease ends on **December 31, 2032**, and the obligation to pay rent begins on **April 1, 2027**.
The period through **March 31, 2027** is rent-free to allow time to prepare, fit out, and organize the business.

Interested parties are asked to read the full tender document carefully.
It sets out the conditions of participation, the required deposit, supporting documents, and the tenant's other obligations.
