Before we even meet for our first Messy Play on Tuesday, August 18, we already have a second colorful experience ready for our children.

Those who would like another dose of Messy Play, as well as those who will not be able to join us for the first event, will have another opportunity for play, exploration, creativity, and plenty of mess.

This time, **Christina Kontogeorgou** and **Tereza Kontogeorgou** will be with us, bringing new art and Messy Play activities.

To keep the dates clear:

- **1st Messy Play:** Tuesday, August 18
- **2nd Messy Play:** Friday, August 21, at 19:00
- **Location:** Tarampados playground

Get ready for another afternoon full of color, imagination, sensory experiences, and children’s smiles.

Do not forget a change of clothes! At Messy Play, getting messy is not an accident — it is part of the fun.

We look forward to seeing you!

**Organized by:** the Cultural Association of Tarampados Tinos
